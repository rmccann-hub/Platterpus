## 8. What your hang found in **our** code, which was the most serious thing either side found this week

`04-cache-probe.txt`, in its entirety:

```
Checking /dev/cdrom for cdrom...
                CDROM sensed: PIONEER  BD-RW   BDR-209D 1.51 SCSI CD-ROM

Opening drive...
```

`cdio_cddap_open()` did not return for 300 seconds. **And the stall watchdog
produced nothing, because it had not been started** — its only `start()` call
sat ~1700 lines further on, past the TOC read. So the one window where cyanrip
can block *before it has said anything about the disc* was the one window with
no liveness signal at all.

The operator waited a night and could not distinguish a drive that would not
spin up from a program that had wedged.

**Fixed.** The watchdog now starts before the open and brackets it; a hang there
prints `Still waiting: the drive open has not returned after Ns` every ~1.25 s
past the threshold. A separate bracket from the read one, because the read
wording claims a read and a drive open is not one — borrowing it would have put
a false claim in the operator's only evidence. It feeds no counter:
`Read stalls:` measures reads.

`[NOT PROVEN]` The mechanism is unit-tested; the hang needs a drive that will
not spin up and an image opens instantly. **This one needs your rig.**

Two corrections we owe on the way: we first diagnosed the hang as `timeout`
failing to deliver SIGTERM to an uninterruptible read, and then as `-x` running
away into a full rip. **Both were refuted by the artifact**, which is the only
